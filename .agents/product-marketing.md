## Product Overview

**One-liner:** UPRAISER is a London performance agency that buys and reconciles mobile growth so Ad Ops and finance read the same file.

**What it does:**  
Buys user acquisition across OEM (Lenovo ROM / Google PAI), in-app programmatic, App Growth (CPI/CPA/FTD), social/search, CTV, native, rewarded, retargeting, and creators — with pre-bid filtration and device-level logs wired into the client’s MMP. Official Lenovo agency partner for procurement-grade OEM alongside performance buying.

**Product category:** Mobile / web user acquisition · performance marketing agency · traffic infrastructure (not antifraud SaaS).

**Product type:** B2B services agency (traffic operator / media buying desk).

**Business model:** Agency / managed buying — pilot → ongoing flights. Lead gen via site contact (`Request Pilot`). Pricing not published on site *[confirm commercial model / retainers]*.

## Problems & Pain Points

**Core problem:**  
Spend scales, but MMP and media bill stop agreeing — and most agencies optimize the dashboard instead of the pipe.

**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Resellers / opaque networks — no device-level trail  
- “OEM” that is open-exchange inventory relabeled  
- Install / CPI vanity that breaks after the flight  
- White-glove reporting that hides gaps until month-end  

**What it costs them:**  
Wasted media, finance distrust, delayed board reporting, compliance risk on regulated verticals.

**Emotional tension:**  
Tired of explaining attribution drift; fear of another war-room month-end; pride in a path procurement and finance will accept.

## Differentiation

**Key differentiators:**
- Dual narrative: **Growth** (scale / revenue) ↔ **Infrastructure** (logs / audit) — same company, two truths  
- **Official Lenovo agency partner** — factory ROM / PAI, not resold exchange  
- Pre-bid fraud filtration + device-level trails into client MMP  
- Caps wired to revenue events finance already trusts  

**How we do it differently:**  
Fix the supply path at auction time so bid-time and bill-time agree — don’t paper over drift in a slide.

**Why that’s better:**  
Month-end becomes uneventful; Ad Ops can defend every dollar; Growth can ramp without breaking unit economics.

**Why customers choose us:**  
*[from site testimonials]* “one file,” “factory-side proof,” “weekly reconciliation calls → monthly file pull.”

## Switching Dynamics

**Push:** Attribution fights with finance; CPA breaks at scale; fake OEM; black-box networks.
